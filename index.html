SCIENTIFIC PRINCIPLE

Culture media combine nutrients and other components selected for a defined educational or laboratory purpose.

EXPECTED OBSERVATION

Your selected medium and current stage will determine what you should observe in this simulation.

COMMON MISTAKE

Treating a generic formulation as universal. Always distinguish illustrative data from a manufacturer's validated instructions.

In real laboratory work, composition and processing affect the medium's properties. A simulation helps you reason about those relationships but cannot validate a real preparation.
